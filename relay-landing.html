What Relay does

The full picture,
from first scan to follow-up complete.

No spreadsheets. No paper cards. No one falling through the cracks after the altar call.

📷

QR badge scanning

Pre-registered attendees get FC badges. Walk-ins get WI badges. Scan any badge and the profile loads instantly.

⚡

One-tap milestones

Baptized, filled with the Spirit, Bible study request — tap the moment it happens. Saves to Planning Center immediately.

🔁

Search-first walk-ins

Forces a PCO search before creating new records. Prevents the duplicate problem that plagues event check-in.

👥

Volunteer assignments

Every milestone holder gets assigned to a volunteer. Contact attempts logged. Status tracked from Unassigned to Completed.

📍

Campus routing

Out-of-area attendees get routed to their nearest campus. Assignment syncs directly to Planning Center's primary_campus field.

🔒

Audit trail

Every assignment, status change, and contact attempt is permanently logged with attribution. Nothing gets lost or quietly changed.

How it works

From first scan
to follow-up closed.

Relay is designed for the chaos of a live outdoor event. Fast, mobile-first, and forgiving when the line is twenty people deep.

1

Scan or register

Pre-registered attendees scan their FC badge. Walk-ins get a WI badge and are registered in under 30 seconds.

2

Record milestones

One tap for baptism, Holy Ghost, or Bible study. Saves directly to Planning Center with your name attached.

3

Sync follow-ups

After the event, leadership assigns every milestone holder to a volunteer. Household info and contact details pulled from PCO.

4

Close the loop

Volunteers log every call, text, and visit. Out-of-area attendees get routed to their nearest campus automatically.
