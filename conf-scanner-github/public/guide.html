Setting up your profile

1

Enter your name and phone number

On first launch a profile prompt appears. Enter your full name and a 10-digit US phone number. This stamps your name on every note you record and lets you recover your profile if your session resets.

2

You're automatically added to the volunteer roster

Opening the Follow-up tab registers you as a volunteer. You'll appear in the assignment dropdown so leadership can assign people to you.

If your cookies clear and you're asked for your profile again, use the same phone number — the app will recognize you and not create a duplicate.
3

Add to your home screen

iPhone: tap Share (□↑) in Safari → Add to Home Screen. Android: tap Menu (⋮) in Chrome → Add to Home screen. This gives you a full-screen app experience.

Two types of badges

FC-001

Pre-Registered

Printed ahead of time with the attendee's name and QR code. Scan and their Planning Center profile loads instantly — no extra steps.

Scan and go
WI-001

Walk-In

Blank badges with only a number and QR code. Keep a stack at the welcome table. Hand one out then check them in through the Walk-ins tab.

Register on arrival

How to check in a walk-in

1

Hand them a WI badge

Grab the next badge from the walk-in stack at the welcome table.

2

Open the Walk-ins tab and scan their badge

Tap the camera icon and scan the WI badge QR code, or type the badge number manually (e.g. WI-014).

3

Search Planning Center first

Type their name in the search field. Many people are already in PCO from previous services. If you find them, tap their name and tap "Link badge & open profile."

Always search before creating. Duplicates in Planning Center create follow-up problems later.
4

If not found — create a new record

Tap "Not in PCO — create new." Enter their name, phone, and email. Their contact info is how we follow up after the crusade — make every effort to collect it.

If the line is long, save with just a name and come back for contact info before the service ends.
5

Tap any milestones that apply

Right on the profile — Baptized, Holy Ghost, or Bible Study. Saves instantly to Planning Center.

6

After the event — sync any offline captures

Anyone saved without a PCO record appears in Walk-ins under Pending PCO Sync. Tap "Push to PCO" for each one before the end of the day.

Recording milestones

On every attendee profile you'll see three milestone buttons. Tap the moment it happens — it saves immediately to Planning Center under the Freedom Crusade category.

Baptized in Jesus Name

Filled with the Holy Ghost

Requested Bible Study

Don't have their info yet? Tap the milestone and save anyway. Go to the Walk-ins tab later to fill in their details and sync to Planning Center.

Follow-up system

After the event, leadership assigns every milestone holder and Bible study request to a volunteer for personal follow-up. All contact attempts are logged and every change is saved permanently.

1

Sync from dashboard

On the Follow-up tab tap "Sync from dashboard" — this pulls in everyone who received a milestone during the crusade and creates their follow-up record automatically.

2

Assign to a volunteer

Tap any person to open their follow-up card. Use the "Assigned to" dropdown to pick a volunteer, then tap Save. Status updates to Assigned automatically.

3

Campus referral for out-of-area attendees

If someone is not from the Syracuse area, use the Campus referral dropdown to assign them to their nearest CLA campus. This saves to Planning Center so the right campus can follow up.

Campuses: CLA Auburn, CLA Geneva, CLA Morrisville, CLA North Syracuse, CLA Oneida, CLA Syracuse.
4

Volunteers log their contact attempts

Open "My assignments" to see your people. Tap a person, log each attempt — Phone call, Text, or Visit — with the outcome and any notes. This builds a permanent contact history for each person.

5

Update contact info if missing

If someone was created without a phone or email, enter it directly in their follow-up card. Tap "Save & sync to PCO" and it updates their Planning Center profile immediately.

6

Mark completed when follow-up is done

Tap the Completed status button when the person has been reached and fully followed up with. The Follow-up tab badge count updates in real time.
